Big Card Solitaire — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-09-24

Big Card Solitaire ("the app") is a solitaire card game published by Rafael ("we"). This policy explains what information the app handles and why.

What the app itself stores

  • Your games and settings stay on your phone. The game in progress, your settings (sound, vibration, draw mode, timer, auto-complete, tap to move), your results (games won, best moves and best time), a few counters that space out full-screen ads (games finished, when the last ad showed) and your place in the list of deals are saved only on your device. The app opts out of Android cloud backup, so none of it leaves the phone. We have no servers and no accounts, and we never receive this information.
  • Uninstalling the app, or clearing its data in Android Settings, deletes all of it.

Advertising

The app is free and shows ads from Google AdMob: a banner at the bottom of the game screen, an occasional full-screen ad between games, and optional video ads you can choose to watch for a hint.

To show and measure these ads, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect and share with Google:

  • your device's advertising ID and app set ID,
  • your approximate location, derived from your IP address,
  • information about the ads you see and tap,
  • diagnostic information about how the SDK performs.

This is described in Google's policy: https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads

Your choices

  • Consent. If you are in the European Economic Area, the UK or Switzerland, or in a US state with privacy laws, the app asks for your consent choices through Google's consent message before any personalised ads are shown. You can change them at any time in the app under Settings › Privacy options.
  • Advertising ID. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in Android Settings › Privacy › Ads.
  • The game works fully without an internet connection; ads simply don't appear offline.

Children

The app is intended for adults and is not directed at children under 13.
