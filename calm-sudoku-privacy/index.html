Calm Sudoku — Privacy Policy

Effective: 2026-09-23

This policy covers Calm Sudoku, a single-player number puzzle for Android. In short: the game keeps your puzzles on your phone, and the only data that leaves the device is what Google's advertising software needs to show ads.

Information kept on your device

Your puzzle in progress, your solved counts and best times, the date of your last Daily puzzle and your settings (sound, vibration, timer, mistake highlighting and similar) are saved locally on your phone. There are no accounts and no Calm Sudoku servers, so none of this is sent to us. Uninstalling the app, or clearing its storage in Android settings, removes it.

Ads and the information Google receives

Calm Sudoku is free because it shows ads from Google AdMob (Google LLC). When an ad is requested or shown, the Google Mobile Ads SDK built into the app may process:

  • the advertising ID of your device (you can reset or delete it under Android Settings → Google → Ads);
  • a rough location worked out from your IP address;
  • ad interactions, such as whether an ad was seen or tapped;
  • diagnostic data about how the ad software is performing.

How Google handles this is explained here:

  • How Google uses data from partner apps: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites
  • Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

Consent and your choices

Where the law requires it — including the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, Switzerland and US states with consumer-privacy laws — the app shows Google's consent message (the User Messaging Platform) before any ad is requested, and uses your answer. You can review or change it at any time under Settings → Privacy options in the app. You can also turn off personalised ads for every app on your device in Android's Google ad settings.

Watching an ad is always your choice for hints: a hint ad opens only when you tap Hint after your free hint is used.

Children

Calm Sudoku is made for adults. It is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.
