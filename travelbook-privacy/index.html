TravelBook — Privacy Policy

Effective: 2026-11-01

This policy covers TravelBook, a travel budget and expense tracker for Android. In short: everything you enter stays on your phone. There are no accounts, no ads and no analytics, and we do not collect any personal data.

Information kept on your device

Your trips (names, cities, dates, budgets, currencies, travel party, members' names and children's age groups), your expenses (amounts, categories, notes, payment method, who paid) and the exchange rates the app has downloaded are saved only in the app's private storage on your phone. There are no TravelBook servers, so none of this is sent to us. The app does not use Android cloud backup. Uninstalling the app, or clearing its storage in Android settings, removes all of it.

Exchange rates

To convert foreign-currency expenses, the app downloads a public table of exchange rates from the free exchange-api project, served by jsDelivr (cdn.jsdelivr.net) and Cloudflare Pages (currency-api.pages.dev). The request contains no information about you or your trips; like any web request, it reveals your device's IP address to those services, which handle it under their own privacy policies. Nothing else is sent over the network.

Sharing

When you tap Share on a trip recap, the app hands the recap text to the app you choose through Android's share sheet. Nothing is shared unless you do this.

Permissions

The app asks only for internet access, which it uses for the exchange rates above.

Children

TravelBook is made for adults planning and tracking trips. It is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from anyone.
