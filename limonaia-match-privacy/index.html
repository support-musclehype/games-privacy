Limonaia Match — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-09-26

Limonaia Match ("the app") is a relaxed match-three puzzle game published by Rafael ("we"). This policy explains what information the app handles and why.

What the app itself stores

  • Everything stays on your phone. Your stars and best score for each level, your unfinished level, your settings (the hint delay, sound, vibration), which tips you have already seen, and a few counters that space out full-screen ads (levels completed, when the last ad showed) are saved only on your device. The app opts out of Android cloud backup, so none of it leaves the phone. We have no servers and no accounts, and we never receive this data.
  • The levels, the hints and the new fruit that drops in are all worked out on your phone.
  • Uninstalling the app, or clearing its data in Android Settings, deletes all of it.

Advertising

The app is free and shows ads from Google AdMob: a banner below the game, an occasional full-screen ad after a completed level, and optional short videos you can choose to watch for five extra moves or for extra Shears.

To show and measure these ads, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect and share with Google your device's advertising ID and app set ID, your approximate location (from your IP address), information about the ads you see and tap, and diagnostic information about the SDK. See https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads

Your choices

  • Consent. In the European Economic Area, the UK, Switzerland and US states with privacy laws, the app asks for your choices through Google's consent message before personalised ads are shown. Change them any time under Settings › Privacy options.
  • Advertising ID. Reset or delete it in Android Settings › Privacy › Ads.
  • The game works fully offline; ads simply don't appear then, and Hint and Try again always work.

Children

The app is intended for adults and is not directed at children under 13.
