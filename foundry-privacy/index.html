Game Privacy Policy

Last updated: 17 October 2026

This policy covers the Android editions of Hushwood Chess, Lido Hearts, Honeyfield Numbers, Kitefield Arrows, Brasswater Pipes, Pennypost Cryptogram, Porchlight Spades and Railyard Slide, published by Rafael Mammadli.

Local game data

Saved games, results, daily progress, streaks, settings and counters used to space out advertising are stored on your phone. These games have no accounts, chat or game server. We do not receive your saved games. Clearing app data or uninstalling removes local data; Android backup is disabled in these editions.

Google Analytics for Firebase

The Android games use Google Analytics for Firebase to understand installation, app openings, sessions and screen activity. Google may process installation and app instance identifiers, device and app information, approximate location derived from IP address, and advertising identifiers where available and permitted. A game_tag property distinguishes each game in our shared analytics configuration. We do not send your name, email, contacts, precise location or saved game to Firebase. No account or sign-in is needed.

These editions do not fetch Firebase Remote Config values and do not include Crashlytics. Read Firebase privacy information and Google partner-site data use.

Google AdMob

The games show a banner, occasional interstitial ads after completed games or puzzles, and optional rewarded hints clearly marked AD. Google Mobile Ads may collect and share IP-derived approximate location, advertising and app set identifiers, app and ad interactions, and diagnostics for advertising, measurement and fraud prevention. Data is encrypted in transit. See Google advertising privacy.

Your choices

Google User Messaging Platform presents regional consent choices when required. Settings > Privacy options appears when Google makes it available. Reset or delete the advertising ID in Android Settings. Core gameplay works offline; ads and new analytics uploads require a connection. Uninstalling stops further collection. For questions about data already processed, contact us; analytics identifiers are installation-based and identifying the installation may require additional information.

Children and changes

These editions are intended for adults and are not directed at children under 13. Changes to this policy will be published here with a new date.
